# Product Overview

## Product Purpose

Describe the primary purpose of the product and the business problem it solves.

Example:

Mission Watch provides operational visibility of flight missions. It allows mission operators to monitor mission execution, track mission status, identify operational deviations, and respond to events that may impact mission objectives.

---

## Users

List the main user groups and briefly describe how they interact with the product.

Example:

- Mission Operators: Monitor active missions and respond to operational alerts.
- Operations Supervisors: Oversee multiple missions and monitor operational performance.
- Mission Coordinators: Review mission status and coordinate actions across operational teams.

---

## Main Capabilities

List the major capabilities currently provided by the product.

Use short bullet points only.

Example:

- Mission Monitoring
- Mission Search
- Operational Alert Management
- Mission Timeline Visualization
- Mission Status Reporting

---

## Important Business Concepts

Define the business terminology that is important for understanding the product.

Use a bullet list.

Example:

- Mission: An operation being monitored by the platform.
- Mission Status: The current operational state of a mission.
- Alert: A condition requiring operational attention.
- Operational Event: A significant occurrence affecting a mission.
- Mission Timeline: Chronological view of mission events.

---

## Scope

Describe what business activities or responsibilities belong to the product and which related activities are handled outside the product.

Use one or more short paragraphs.

Example:

Mission Watch provides visibility and monitoring capabilities for flight missions. It supports operational awareness, status monitoring, alerting, and mission tracking.

Mission planning, air traffic control operations, and aircraft onboard systems are managed by other systems and are outside the scope of Mission Watch.
